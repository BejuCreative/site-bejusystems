The numbers decide what we mine next

01Moments

We mine your best moments.

Your podcasts, webinars, lives and talks already hold the content. We go through every long-form recording, line by line. Each moment is scored for hook, story and payoff, and checked against the offer you sell. Only the moments that can stop a stranger mid-scroll make the cut.

  • A full transcript and timeline of every recording
  • Moments ranked for hook, story and payoff
  • Every pick tied to a topic your buyers care about

The strongest moments go straight to the edit.

02Shorts

We craft Shorts that stop the scroll.

Every Short is built for the first two seconds. We cut the hook to the front, re-frame you for vertical, and pace the captions word by word. Then the polish: motion graphics, b-roll, sound design, and a clean end card with one clear call to action.

  • Hook-first edit, re-framed for vertical
  • Captions, motion graphics and sound design
  • One call to action that points into your funnel

Every Short ships with a keyword that starts the funnel.

03Posting

We post natively, everywhere.

Each Short goes out as if it was made for that platform alone. Its own caption. Its own cover. Its own time slot. We plan the calendar around your offer, so every week of content builds toward the thing you sell, not just toward views.

  • A content calendar planned around your offer
  • Platform-native captions, covers and timing
  • A keyword call to action on every post

Viewers who want more comment the keyword.

04DM funnel

The DM keyword funnel.

A viewer comments one word. Seconds later, a message lands in their inbox with the free gift they asked for. Everyone who asks gets it. No hoops, and no follow required: we invite the follow, we never make it a condition. Then one question: “Want the full version?” One reply with their email, and a viewer becomes a lead you own.

  • A keyword triggers an instant DM with the gift
  • The gift goes to everyone. The follow is invited, never required.
  • One question turns the chat into a captured email

Every email is tagged with what they asked for.

05Emails

The email engine.

The moment an email lands, the sequence starts. A welcome that delivers on the promise. A run of nurture emails that teach, tell stories and answer the doubts people have before they buy. Then a clear invitation to the next step: the webinar, the program, the book, the community. When you launch, we run the whole campaign: the run-up, the doors-open emails, the reminders and the close. Every lead is segmented by what they asked for, so each one hears about the thing they actually want.

  • Welcome, nurture, launch and invitation emails, written for you
  • Segments by keyword, topic and interest
  • Each email moves the reader one step closer to buying

Every invitation points to one page: the right one for that offer.

06Sales pages

A page for everything you sell.

Readers land on a page built to answer their questions before they ask them: who it's for, what changes, how it works, what happens next. One button, pointed at the product: join the program, save a webinar seat, pre-order the book, join the community, or book a call if you sell 1:1. Confirmations, reminders and welcome emails go out for you, with the lead's full story attached.

  • A sales or sign-up page for each offer
  • Checkout, registration or booking, connected to your tools
  • Confirmations, reminders and welcome emails, sent automatically

Every buyer lands in your CRM, with their history.

07CRM

Your CRM, built around how you sell.

One place for every lead, buyer and sale, sorted by product. Each person carries their whole story: the Short they watched, the word they commented, the webinar they joined, what they bought. Every card has a next step and an owner, so nothing waits in an inbox and nobody slips through the cracks.

  • A pipeline shaped around your sales process
  • Buyers per product: program, book, community, event, 1:1
  • A next step on every card, so follow-ups happen

Every stage feeds the weekly numbers.

08Numbers

Weekly numbers. Weekly changes.

Every week you get one clear report. Which Shorts pulled leads. Which keywords converted. Which emails got replies. How many people signed up, showed up and bought, product by product. Then the part that matters: what we change next week, and why. The system never sits still. We keep tuning it, week after week.

  • A one-page weekly report, in plain words
  • What converted, from first view to sale
  • A short list of changes for next week, then we make them

The winners shape what we mine next. The loop starts again.

Questions.

How do you bring me clients?

We turn your long videos into Shorts and post them on every platform. Each Short invites viewers to comment one word for a free gift. They get it in their DMs, leave their email, and our emails in your voice invite them to buy or book a call. Every lead lands in your CRM, and you see it all in a weekly report.

What happens in the first week?

We map your accounts and pages, list the leaks, and start posting. We build your free gift and DM funnel and write your welcome emails. You approve the first video and the emails. That's your part.

How much does it cost?

Every system is quoted after a call. The price depends on what we build, how much content it needs and which tools you already use.

Is this only about booking calls?

No. Calls are the smallest piece. We point the system at whatever you sell: program and course enrolments, webinar and event registrations, book launches, new community members, workshop and cohort seats, and 1:1 calls if you sell that way.

What do you need from me?

Your long-form content (podcasts, webinars, lives or talks), access to the tools you use, and your say on the plan. We handle the rest, and check in with you on the big calls.

Which tools do you use?

We build on what you already use where we can, for example your course platform or your email tool. If you have nothing yet, we set it up for you.

Can I start with one part?

Yes. Many clients start with the part that leaks the most, then add the rest. The map from the first call shows where to start.

Do people have to follow me to get the free gift?

No. Everyone who comments the keyword gets the gift. We invite a follow in the message, but it is never a condition. That keeps the funnel within platform rules, and it feels better for the person asking.

Do you work with health and wellness experts?

Yes. We keep every word within what your field allows: no clinical claims and no promised outcomes. You review anything sensitive before it goes out.

Is my data kept private?

Yes. Your content, your leads and your customer data stay yours. Access is shared only with the people who build your system.

How do I start?

Book a call. We map your funnel together and tell you what we would build first.
